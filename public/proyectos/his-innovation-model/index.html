The problem it solves

Most industrial research ideas die between the whiteboard and the first working prototype

Industrial R&D teams, academic researchers, and startups routinely generate solid technical ideas that never get validated because building even a rough proof-of-concept takes weeks of setup before anyone can tell if the idea actually works.

Generic "AI coding" doesn't close that gap by itself — unsupervised, it produces plausible-looking prototypes that fall apart under real engineering constraints. The HIS Model exists to direct that capability with actual domain judgment instead of letting it freewheel.

Methodology

Three pillars, applied in sequence

01

AI-assisted systematic research

Automated literature synthesis, technology scouting, and data extraction — structured around the specific technical constraints of the industrial problem at hand, not generic prompts.

02

Rapid prototyping & MVP acceleration

Conceptual specs become functional web apps, dashboards, or simulation models in 48–72 hours, refined through fast AI-guided feedback loops for validation, testing, and polish.

03

Technology transfer & feasibility

Technology readiness level (TRL) evaluation and business-model formulation, so a working prototype comes with an honest read on whether it's commercially viable — not just technically neat.

What "vibe engineering" means here

Direction, not delegation

The AI does the typing; the engineering judgment stays human. Every prototyping cycle runs against explicit domain constraints — industrial engineering limits, feasibility checks, statistical validity — rather than accepting whatever an AI agent produces on its own. That discipline is what turns "AI wrote some code" into a prototype an R&D team can actually evaluate.
